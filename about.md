# About Me

Hey there! My name is Leonardo, but call me Leo. I'm a software engineer, deeply passionate about creating things and about making the world a more amazing place. I believe coding is magic: we turn thin air (okay, not exactly thin air, more like electrons and bytes) into real, impactful change in the world. Information moves fast, and I love the hard challenges that come with it.

I started coding early, at 13 (so, more than 20 years of coding now), and I was extremely lucky to find what I love to do so young. My hobby is coding. My work is coding.

Speaking of work, I'm currently at Shopify, on the Convert Explore team, where we dive into the depths of creation to find more amazing ways to bring new merchants onto our platform. Some of my recent work includes speeding up signup to 3.5 seconds, [GMV Rewards](https://www.shopify.com/ca/gmv-promotion/terms-and-conditions){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}, and the [Agentic Plan](https://www.shopify.com/agentic-plan){:target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"}. And we're cooking much, much more.

This blog is where I share my thoughts and, hopefully, teach you a thing or two along the way.
